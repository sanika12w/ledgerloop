# ECONOMICS.md — Unit Economics & Growth Model

## 1. Value of a Converted Lead (Credex Context)

A “converted lead” = a startup that:
- completes an AI stack audit
- books a Credex consultation
- is identified as high AI spend inefficiency
- is a candidate for AI infrastructure / credit optimization (Credex upsell)

### Assumptions:
- Avg startup AI spend identified: **$300–$2,000/month waste or inefficiency**
- Credex monetization: takes **10–20% optimization/credit margin**
- Typical contract value influenced by audit: **$3,000–$15,000/year per company**

### Estimated value per converted lead:
- Low case: $3,000 ARR
- Realistic case: $6,000–$8,000 ARR
- High case: $12,000+ ARR (high AI usage startups)

👉 **Working estimate: ~$6,500 ARR per converted lead**

---

## 2. CAC by Channel (from GTM plan)

### (A) Founder DM / Manual Outreach
- Cost: $0 cash, high time cost
- Conversion:
  - 100 DMs → ~10 replies → ~5 audits → ~1–2 conversions
- CAC: **~$0–$30 (time-adjusted)**

---

### (B) Community Posts (Reddit, Indie Hackers, Slack)
- Cost: $0
- Conversion:
  - 5,000 impressions → 50–100 clicks → 10 audits → 2–3 conversions
- CAC: **~$10–$50 equivalent (time + effort)**

---

### (C) Viral Audit Links (Organic sharing inside startups)
- Cost: $0
- Conversion:
  - 1 audit → 2–3 internal views → 1 potential inbound lead
- CAC: **~$0–$5 (best channel)**

---

### Blended CAC estimate:
👉 **$10–$40 per audit-driven lead**

---

## 3. Funnel Conversion Assumptions

### Funnel:
Audit Completed → Credex Call → Paid Customer

- Audit → Call booked: **10–20%**
- Call → Credit purchase: **25–40%**
- Overall audit → purchase: **~3–8%**

---

### Example math (per 100 audits):

- 100 audits completed
- 15 book Credex call
- 5–6 convert into paid customers

👉 Conversion rate:
**~5% average**

---

## 4. Profitability Model

### Revenue per 100 audits:
- 5 conversions × $6,500 ARR = **$32,500 ARR**

### Cost per 100 audits:
- CAC ~ $20 per audit lead
- 100 audits → **~$2,000 acquisition cost (time-adjusted)**

👉 Gross efficiency:
- $32,500 revenue / $2,000 cost
- **~16x return on acquisition**

To reach $1M ARR:

### Required customers:

- → ~155 paying customers

---

### Required funnel scale:

If conversion rate = 5%:
- Need ~3,100 audits completed

---

### Traffic requirement:

Assuming:
- 20% audit completion rate from visitors

Then:
- Need ~15,000 targeted users total

---

- ~9 customers/month
- ~180 audits/month

---

## 6. Growth Logic (Critical Insight)

This model does NOT scale like SaaS SEO funnels.

It scales like:
- high-trust audit distribution
- viral internal sharing inside startups
- founder-to-founder referral loops

---

## Key Dependency

This only works if:
- audits are perceived as “money-saving events”, not dashboards
- results are specific enough to trigger internal Slack sharing
- Credex offer is positioned as “instant optimization layer”, not sales-heavy upsell

---

## Bottom Line

- Low CAC ($10–$40 range)
- High perceived value ($3k–$15k per lead)
- Strong funnel conversion (~5%)
- Best scaling driver = virality inside companies, not paid acquisition
